## Project Structure & Page Flow

### 1. Front Matter & Introduction

* **Title Page:** Project title, subject (Physical Education), your name, roll number, class/section, and academic year.
* **Certificate of Authenticity:** Standard PE project certificate section signed by your teacher/examiner.
* **Acknowledgement:** A brief section thanking your PE teacher and school principal.
* **Index Page:** Include columns for *S.No.*, *Topic Name*, *Page Numbers*, and *Teacher's Remarks*.
* **Introduction to Adaptive Sports:** A short introductory section explaining what adaptive/inclusive sports are and why these three major games exist.

### 2. Standardized Section Layout (For Each Event)

To make your notebook look consistent and well-organized, follow the exact same sub-header structure for **Special Olympics**, **Paralympics**, and **Deaflympics**:

1. **Overview & Definition**
2. **History & Origin** *(Founder, first games location, year established)*
3. **Governing Body** *(e.g., IOC, IPC, SOI, CISM)*
4. **Eligibility Criteria** *(Who can participate - types of impairment/disabilities served)*
5. **Motto, Aim & Oath**
6. **Symbol, Flag & Flame / Torch Relay**
7. **Major Events & Sports Included** *(Categorized into Summer vs. Winter sports)*
8. **Frequency & Year Cycle** *(How often held and recent/upcoming host cities)*
9. **Notable Athletes / Key Figures** *(1–2 famous Indian and global athletes)*

### 3. Concluding & Synthesis Sections

* **Comparison Table:** A summary matrix comparing Special Olympics, Paralympics, and Deaflympics side-by-side across key parameters (target audience, governing body, motto, first year).
* **India's Performance & Achievements:** Highlights of India's participation and medal counts in recent editions of each game.
* **Impact of Adaptive Sports:** Benefits of these movements on social inclusion, physical fitness, and awareness.
* **Bibliography / References:** Books, textbooks, or official websites referenced.

## 2. Game (Example: Basketball)

* **Overview:** A fast-paced team sport played on a rectangular court where two teams of 5 active players compete to score points by shooting a ball through an elevated hoop (10 feet high).
* **Key Court Specifications:**
* Court Size: $28\text{ m} \times 15\text{ m}$
* Ring Height: $3.05\text{ m}$ (10 ft)


* **Fundamental Skills:** Dribbling, Passing (Chest pass, Bounce pass), Shooting (Layup, Jump shot), and Rebounding.
* **Basic Rules:** 4 quarters of 10 minutes each (FIBA standard); players move the ball by bouncing (dribbling) or passing; illegal movement without bouncing is called a *travelling* violation.

## 3. Khelo India Programme (Fitness Test Battery)

The Sports Authority of India (SAI) under the Khelo India scheme developed a standardized fitness test battery divided by age group:

### Age Group: 5–8 Years (Classes 1 to 3)

*Focuses on Fundamental Movement Skills (FMS).*

1. **BMI (Body Mass Index):** Height and weight calculation to track growth and body composition.
2. **Plate Tapping Test:** Measures hand-eye coordination and reaction speed.
3. **Flamingo Balance Test:** Single-leg balance test to assess body control and leg strength.

### Age Group: 9–18+ Years (Classes 4 to 12)

*Focuses on physical health components and components of fitness.*

1. **BMI (Body Mass Index):** Assesses body composition.
2. **50m Dash (Sprint):** Measures top speed and acceleration.
3. **600m Run/Walk:** Measures cardiovascular endurance and stamina.
4. **Sit and Reach Test:** Measures flexibility of the lower back and hamstrings.
5. **Partial Curl-Up (30 Seconds):** Measures abdominal strength and muscular endurance.
6. **Push-Ups (Boys) / Modified Push-Ups (Girls):** Measures upper-body strength and endurance.
